## 4. Beginner-Friendly Explainer

### What this artifact does
This document is a standardized Usability Observation and Results Sheet. It tracks quantitative task metrics (success rate, duration, error rate) and qualitative behavioral observations (hesitations, misinterpretations, quotes) for each participant across all 5 test tasks.

### Why it matters
In product design, quantitative data tells you *what* happened, while qualitative observation tells you *why* it happened. Tracking task times, error counts, and assistance tiers transforms subjective feedback into objective, prioritizable design improvements.

### How to use it
- Print or keep this document open during live usability testing sessions.
- Record timestamps when a participant starts and finishes each task.
- Log exact quotes and classify assistance tiers immediately.
- Tally success rates and median times in the executive summary table once all sessions are complete.

### What I can honestly claim in an interview
- **Can claim:** You engineered this standardized usability testing scorecard, established objective task success criteria, and created an audit trail linking usability flaws directly to design backlog tickets.
- **Cannot claim:** You cannot claim that any of these tasks have been completed by real users. All metrics, durations, and observations are explicitly marked `[TO FILL AFTER REAL TESTS]`.
