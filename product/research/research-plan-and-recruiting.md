## 1. Research Objectives & Problem Statement

While quantitative review mining (426 Google Play reviews) established that synchronization and Bluetooth connectivity drive 42.9% of negative ratings in our sample, app store reviews suffer from inherent selection bias and lack behavioral granularity.

This research study is designed to conduct generative, semi-structured user interviews with active wearable users to:
1. Understand the daily mental models and emotional habits surrounding health data synchronization.
2. Observe how users naturally detect delays, missing metrics, or broken step streaks.
3. Investigate the self-initiated troubleshooting behaviors, workarounds, and unpairing patterns users employ when companion apps fail to sync.
4. Validate whether the 4-stage conceptual model (**Detect $\rightarrow$ Explain $\rightarrow$ Recover $\rightarrow$ Confirm**) matches genuine user troubleshooting mental models.

## 2. Participant Profile & Sample Composition

- **Target Sample Size:** 5 to 8 participants ($N=5\text{--}8$). In qualitative generative research, 5 to 8 interviews reliably surface $\sim 80\text{--}85\%$ of primary usability themes and behavioral friction points without diminishing returns.
- **Participant Composition:**
  - 3–4 Noise smartwatch owners (e.g., ColorFit Pro series, Noise Voyage, Pulse series).
  - 2–4 owners of competing mainstream fitness wearables (boAt, Fitbit/Google, Amazfit, Samsung Galaxy Watch).
  - Balanced split of smartphone operating systems (at least 3 Android users and at least 2 iOS users).
  - Diverse daily activity levels (from casual 5,000-step walkers to intensive daily workout trackers).

---

## 3. Inclusion & Exclusion Criteria

### Inclusion Criteria
- Owns and wears a smartwatch or fitness tracker at least 5 days per week.
- Has paired the wearable with a companion smartphone application for at least 30 days.
- Regularly views activity, step, sleep, or workout metrics on their mobile app.
- Age 18 or older.
- Provides voluntary informed consent for a 30-minute interview and note-taking.

### Exclusion Criteria
- Individuals employed by wearable hardware manufacturers (Noise, boAt, Apple, Google/Fitbit, Samsung, Garmin).
- Professional software engineers or QA testers who work directly on Bluetooth Low Energy or mobile companion apps (to avoid technical bias).
- Individuals who only use basic digital wristwatches without mobile app pairing.
- Refusal to consent to confidential research note-taking.

## 4. Participant Screener Questionnaire

> **Recruitment Integrity Rule:** Screener questions must remain completely neutral. Never ask leading questions such as *"Have you experienced annoying sync errors in NoiseFit?"* which bias the participant pool toward pre-conceived complaints.

```markdown
1. Which brand and model of smartwatch or fitness tracker do you currently wear?
   [ ] Noise (Please specify model: ________________)
   [ ] boAt
   [ ] Fitbit / Google
   [ ] Samsung
   [ ] Apple
   [ ] Other: ________________
   [ ] I do not currently wear a smartwatch (Disqualify)

2. On average, how many days per week do you wear your tracker?
   [ ] Less than 3 days per week (Disqualify)
   [ ] 3–4 days per week
   [ ] 5–7 days per week (Target)

3. Which operating system does your primary smartphone use?
   [ ] Android (Version / Phone Model: ________________)
   [ ] iOS (iPhone Model: ________________)
   [ ] Other: ________________

4. How often do you open your wearable's companion app on your phone?
   [ ] Multiple times per day
   [ ] Once a day
   [ ] A few times a week
   [ ] Rarely / Never (Disqualify)

5. Which features do you look at most frequently in your companion app? (Select all that apply)
   [ ] Step counts & daily activity streaks
   [ ] Sleep duration & sleep stages
   [ ] Heart rate & workout tracking
   [ ] Watch faces & appearance
   [ ] Notifications & calls
   [ ] Other: ________________

6. Have you paired or synchronized your watch with your phone within the past 7 days?
   [ ] Yes
   [ ] No (Disqualify)
```

## 6. Privacy, Ethics & Data Collection Boundaries

1. **Zero Biometric Health Data Collection:** The researcher must NEVER ask for, record, or store participant medical records, precise step counts, heart rate readings, sleep staging data, or GPS workout locations.
2. **Pseudonymization:** All participant records must be assigned an alphanumeric ID (`P-01` to `P-08`). Names, email addresses, and phone numbers must be stored in a separate, password-protected recruitment sheet and destroyed upon study completion.
3. **Right to Withdraw:** Participants may pause the interview, skip any question, or end the session at any moment without penalty.
4. **Recording Policy:** Audio or video recording requires explicit, recorded verbal consent at the beginning of the call. If consent is withheld, the researcher proceeds with written notes only.

## 8. Beginner-Friendly Explainer

### What this artifact does
This document is a complete User Research Plan and Recruiting Screener. It establishes why we conduct research, defines target user profiles, provides a neutral screening survey to filter eligible candidates, includes outreach messaging templates, outlines privacy ethics, and sets up a participant tracking sheet.

### Why it matters
Product Managers must base their roadmaps on validated user needs. An interview plan ensures that user recruitment is methodical, unbiased, and privacy-compliant. Showing a recruiting screener proves that you know how to find the right users without asking leading questions that contaminate the data.

### How to use it
- Use the screener questions in Google Forms or Typeform to recruit wearable users.
- Use the outreach scripts when contacting potential candidates on LinkedIn or community forums.
- Keep the participant tracker updated as you schedule and complete sessions.

### What I can honestly claim in an interview
- **Can claim:** You designed the full research methodology, developed neutral screening questions, authored outreach scripts, and structured the compliance framework for a 5–8 participant qualitative study.
- **Cannot claim:** You cannot claim that you have already recruited or interviewed these participants. All rows in the tracker remain honestly marked `[TO FILL]`.
