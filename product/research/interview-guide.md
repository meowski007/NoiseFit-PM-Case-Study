## 1. Facilitator Protocol & Ethics Script

### 1.1. Introduction & Welcome (2 Minutes)
> *"Hello [Participant Name], thank you so much for taking the time to speak with me today! My name is [Researcher Name], and I am conducting an independent product research study focused on how people use their smartwatches and mobile companion apps to track their daily health and fitness.  
> Before we begin, I want to clarify that there are no right or wrong answers. I am not testing you or your technical knowledge; I am simply trying to understand your honest, everyday experiences—what works smoothly, what feels frustrating, and how you interact with your devices.  
> Your feedback will be used purely for educational product management analysis."*

### 1.2. Informed Consent, Privacy & Recording Permission (2 Minutes)
> *"To ensure your comfort, here are a few ground rules:  
> 1. **Confidentiality:** Everything you share is completely confidential. Your name will never be published; all notes will be anonymized under an ID code like P-01.  
> 2. **Privacy:** I will not ask for or record any sensitive personal data, passwords, or specific medical health readings.  
> 3. **Voluntary Participation:** You have the right to skip any question you prefer not to answer, and you can pause or end our conversation at any time.  
> With your permission, I would like to record this session solely to ensure my notes accurately reflect your words. The recording will not be shared externally. Are you comfortable with me recording today's call?"*  
> `[ ] Yes, consent granted` | `[ ] No, notes only`

## 2. Warm-Up & Contextual Grounding (3–5 Minutes)

- **Goal:** Put the participant at ease and establish their everyday wearable usage baseline without priming them on synchronization.
- **Introductory Questions:**
  - *"To start off, could you tell me a little bit about the smartwatch or fitness tracker you wear every day? How long have you had it?"*
  - *"What are the primary things you rely on it for during a typical weekday versus a weekend?"*
  - *"When do you usually look at your watch screen versus opening the companion app on your phone?"*

---

## 3. Core Behavioral Questions & Neutral Probes (20–25 Minutes)

> **Interview Technique Note:** Questions are strictly anchored in **actual past events** rather than hypothetical opinions. We deliberately avoid asking *"Do you want a Sync Center?"* or *"Do you hate sync errors?"* to eliminate confirmation bias.

---

### Question 1: Recent Wearable Syncing Experiences
*"Think back to the last couple of times you opened your companion app to look at your daily activity or workout data. Walk me through what happened from the moment you unlocked your phone."*

- **Neutral Probes:**
  - *"How long did it take for your latest steps or workout to appear on the screen?"*
  - *"What visual cues did you see on the screen while the app was updating?"*
  - *"Did you have to do anything manually, or did the data just show up?"*

---

### Question 2: How the Participant Noticed a Sync Problem
*"Can you describe a specific time recently when your smartwatch data didn't appear in the app as you expected?"*

- **Neutral Probes:**
  - *"Where were you, and what were you trying to check at that moment (e.g., steps, sleep, a run)?"*
  - *"How did you first realize that something was delayed or missing?"*
  - *"Did the app give you any alert or message, or did you have to figure it out yourself?"*
  - *"How did that moment make you feel regarding your activity streak or health record?"*

---

### Question 3: What They Did Immediately Next
*"Once you realized the data wasn't there or was outdated, what was the very first action you took?"*

- **Neutral Probes:**
  - *"Did you wait, or did you try interacting with the app right away?"*
  - *"Did you pull down on the screen to refresh? How many times did you try that?"*
  - *"What did the screen show while you were doing that?"*

---

### Question 4: How They Diagnosed the Issue
*"In that situation, what did you think was causing the delay or failure?"*

- **Neutral Probes:**
  - *"Did the app provide any explanation of what was happening?"*
  - *"If an error message appeared, what did it say? How clear or helpful was that message?"*
  - *"Did you think the issue was with your watch, your phone, Bluetooth, the internet, or the app itself? What made you think that?"*

---

### Question 5: What Recovery Actions They Tried
*"When the initial refresh didn't solve the problem, what other steps did you try to get your data to transfer?"*

- **Neutral Probes:**
  - *"Did you toggle Bluetooth, turn Airplane mode on/off, or close the app completely?"*
  - *"Did you restart your phone or power-cycle your watch?"*
  - *"Have you ever unpaired your watch or reinstalled the companion app? What led you to take that step?"*

---

### Question 6: How They Knew Whether Recovery Worked
*"Once you finished trying those troubleshooting steps, how did you verify whether your data actually transferred successfully?"*

- **Neutral Probes:**
  - *"Did the app display an explicit confirmation, or did you have to click through individual tabs (like Sleep or Heart Rate) to check?"*
  - *"Did all of your data come back, or did some categories stay missing (for instance, steps synced but sleep didn't)?"*
  - *"At what point did you feel confident that your records were safe?"*

---

### Question 7: Workarounds and Their Consequences
*"When synchronization issues couldn't be fixed right away, what did you do? How did you adapt?"*

- **Neutral Probes:**
  - *"Did you lose any step streaks, workout milestones, or sleep history as a result?"*
  - *"Did you ever contact customer support or visit an online forum? What was that experience like?"*
  - *"Did that experience change how often you wear your watch or how much you trust the numbers on your dashboard?"*

---

### Question 8: Expectations and Unmet Needs
*"Looking back at your overall experience with your wearable, what is the single most important thing a companion app should do when something goes wrong with data syncing?"*

- **Neutral Probes:**
  - *"If you could change one thing about how your app handles connection hiccups, what would it be?"*
  - *"How would you prefer to be notified if your watch hasn't synced in several hours?"*
  - *"What kind of feedback gives you peace of mind that your data is intact?"*

## 6. Beginner-Friendly Explainer

### What this artifact does
This interview guide is a complete facilitator script containing introduction wording, informed consent protocols, eight behavioral questions with neutral probes, and a closing script.

### Why it matters
Leading questions (e.g., *"Don't you hate it when Bluetooth disconnects?"*) ruin research validity because participants naturally agree with the interviewer (acquiescence bias). By focusing strictly on *actual past actions* (*"Walk me through what happened..."*), you uncover authentic user pain points rather than polite agreement.

### How to use it
- Keep this guide open on your screen during Zoom or Google Meet interviews.
- Use the neutral probes when a participant gives a brief, one-word answer.
- Never mention the proposed "Sync Center" solution during the interview; let the participant express their own unmet needs.

### What I can honestly claim in an interview
- **Can claim:** You developed a rigorous, non-leading qualitative interview guide mapping the full user troubleshooting journey across Detect, Explain, Recover, and Confirm.
- **Cannot claim:** You cannot claim that you have already interviewed users with this guide until the sessions are completed and logged in the notes template.
