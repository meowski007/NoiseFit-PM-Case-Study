## 4. Beginner-Friendly Explainer

### What this artifact does
This template is a structured field notebook for recording participant responses during qualitative user interviews. It separates what the user literally said (direct quotes), what they did (behavior), and what the researcher infers (interpretation).

### Why it matters
In research, researcher bias is a major hazard. If a PM writes *"Participant hated the Bluetooth screen"* when the user actually said *"I couldn't find the toggle"*, the team solves the wrong problem. Distinguishing quotes from interpretations ensures rigorous data integrity.

### How to use it
- Duplicate this template for each participant (`notes-P01.md`, `notes-P02.md`).
- Fill in metadata before the call starts.
- Type notes directly into the form during the interview or immediately after reviewing the recording.

### What I can honestly claim in an interview
- **Can claim:** You engineered a professional qualitative note-taking framework that separates objective observation from subjective synthesis to guard against cognitive bias.
- **Cannot claim:** You cannot claim that any of the filled-in fields represent completed interviews. All response sections remain explicitly marked `[TO FILL AFTER REAL INTERVIEW]`.
