Confirm

PM Interview Defense Guide

Direct, honest answers prepared for senior leadership and technical interviews at ArisInfra.

Q1: "Why ArisInfra, and how do wearable sync problems translate to construction logistics?"

"ArisInfra is tackling India's multi-billion dollar construction procurement problem. In construction, materials delivery is fundamentally an asynchronous edge workflow: transit mixers and steel trucks arrive at remote job sites or basement basements with zero cellular coverage. If a supervisor cannot log receipt against purchase orders, or if weights mismatch, the company suffers billing disputes. The systems thinking from NoiseFit (offline queueing, 4-stage recovery) and SyncOps (telemetry validation, anomaly detection) directly translates to making jobsite procurement simple, reliable, and auditable."

Q2: "Why TF-IDF rather than dense vector embeddings in your incident triage?"

"In incident triage, operational runbooks are indexed by exact alphanumeric error codes—like AUTH_002 or ERR_TIMEOUT_GATEWAY. Dense vector embeddings tend to soften specific token matches in favor of semantic similarity, which can match the wrong runbook for distinct error codes. TF-IDF guarantees exact-match priority, runs in sub-millisecond offline time, and requires zero paid API dependencies. I added OpenAI tool-calling as an optional synthesis layer, but the foundation is completely deterministic."

Q3: "What are the limitations of your portfolio work?"

"I believe in radical intellectual honesty. In SyncOps, the 5,000 sync events are synthetic, generated with seasonal patterns—so while our AutoARIMA MAE of 0.968 demonstrates correct walk-forward validation methodology, it does not guarantee production generalization. In NoiseFit, the 426 reviews were a purposive sample across rating tiers, and my A/B test plan is an unexecuted experimental design ($N=0$ live users). At ArisInfra, my first priority would be grounding hypotheses in real operational data: shadowing site supervisors and analyzing actual procurement drop-off logs."
